FlowSense estimates river discharge from Sentinel-2 imagery alone, which makes it usable
in basins where no ground gauge exists. The current model is validated against 61 public
gauge stations.

## Status notes

The validation study is complete. Funding is needed to run the two flood-season
deployments and to keep the station-level notebook maintained.
