TerraScope ingests daily imagery from three small-satellite providers and emits change
polygons with a confidence band, so planners can audit every alert back to the source
scene.

## Status notes

The detection models are trained and the alert thresholds are stable. What is still
missing is the budget for two years of ground-truth field campaigns and the cloud
processing quota that the regional agencies need.
