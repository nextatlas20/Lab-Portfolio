LumenViz renders categorical series with pattern and luminance cues so that charts stay
readable under all common colour-vision deficiencies, including monochromatic print.

## Status notes

The library ships as a small renderer, but the accessibility audit, the screen-reader
test suite and the print-compatibility matrix all need paid reviewer time.
