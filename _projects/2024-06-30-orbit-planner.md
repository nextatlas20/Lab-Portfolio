OrbitPlanner schedules imaging passes for constellations of up to forty CubeSats under
power, downlink and weather constraints, and explains every rejected pass so operators
can defend the plan.

## Status notes

The solver is used internally by two partner labs. A funded programme would let us
harden the scheduler and publish the pass-level benchmarks.
