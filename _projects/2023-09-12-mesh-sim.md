MeshSim converts heterogeneous CAD exports into watertight simulation meshes and records
every intermediate artefact, so a simulation run can be rebuilt from the manifest alone.

## Maintenance

The toolkit is in maintenance mode. We accept bug reports and keep the regression suite
green against the three solver versions that field teams still deploy.
